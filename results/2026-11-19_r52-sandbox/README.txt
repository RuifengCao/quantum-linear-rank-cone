The method. For a five-party ray r with pure-symmetric extension S and lift h_S (Theorem 1 of the paper), any binary
matrix M with h_S(E) rows and column groups A..F of widths h_S(i) = 2 S(i) whose column-group ranks are h_S gives the
CSS state of the row space of M, with entropy vector 2S (R32 note, proof of Theorem 1, step 2, and the remark
"symplectic structure is irrelevant"): a qubit realisation of r at lambda = 2. Every realisation at lambda = 1 gives
such a matrix ([I | W] for a graph state W), so the search runs in a space of the size of the lambda = 1 graph-state
search while it finds realisations at lambda = 2.
